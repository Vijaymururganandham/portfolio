Five years ago, I started in EdTech sales — closing deals, understanding what makes people say "yes." That experience taught me something most SEO courses miss: technical excellence means nothing without understanding human behaviour.

"Search intent is just human behaviour at scale. Once you understand that, the algorithm becomes a tool — not the goal."

Today, I bring that same mindset to every project. Whether I'm recovering a site from a black-hat attack, building content clusters for Web3 startups, or turning a 13% conversion rate into 30% — the approach is the same: strategy first, tactics second.

I've worked across two genuinely different worlds: UK and EU restaurant businesses, where local precision and multi-domain management are everything — and Web3 blockchain SaaS, where you're competing for a global audience across the US, China, Singapore, and Russia in one of the most volatile search verticals on the internet.

Both taught me that sustainable rankings come from understanding the business first, then the algorithm. I don't chase metrics in isolation. I build systems that compound.
